work_en: "Based on the LogSAD multi-granularity anomaly detection framework, integrating CLIP, DINOv2, and BEiT multi-source visual features, designing training-free multi-feature projection and normal support feature space reconstruction methods to enhance few-shot normal pattern representation. Further introducing zero-shot anomaly detection priors to enhance global and fine-grained feature expression, combined with regularized post-processing, multi-scale feature extraction, and inference acceleration strategies, ultimately improving Avg Image Score from 81.54% baseline to 93.61%."
