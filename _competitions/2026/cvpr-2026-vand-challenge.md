project_content_en: "Addressing large-scale visual defect detection in real retail logistics scenarios. Based on the Kaputt 2 dataset, identifying product packaging and product anomalies, focusing on solving practical challenges such as highly diverse product categories, significant pose and appearance variations, subtle defects, and noisy reference samples, examining models' robust anomaly recognition capabilities in complex open scenarios."
work: "构建基于 DINOv2/DINOv3 视觉基础模型的多模型缺陷检测框架，采用渐进式解冻实现领域适配，并设计半监督伪标签学习、噪声鲁棒损失及多任务缺陷类型学习提升细粒度异常表征；进一步结合多尺度 TTA、参考图像相似度建模与多模型集成增强预测鲁棒性，最终在 Kaputt 2 测试集取得 90.84% AP 和 94.76% AUROC。"
work_en: "Building a multi-model defect detection framework based on DINOv2/DINOv3 vision foundation models, using progressive unfreezing for domain adaptation, and designing semi-supervised pseudo-label learning, noise-robust loss, and multi-task defect type learning to enhance fine-grained anomaly representation. Further combining multi-scale TTA, reference image similarity modeling, and multi-model ensemble to enhance prediction robustness, ultimately achieving 90.84% AP and 94.76% AUROC on Kaputt 2 test set."
