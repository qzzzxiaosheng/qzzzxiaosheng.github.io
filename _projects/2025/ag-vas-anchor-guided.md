work_en: "Proposing an Anchor-Guided zero-shot anomaly segmentation framework with absolute anchors [SEG] and relative anchors [NOR]/[ANO] to transform abstract anomaly concepts into learnable visual localization representations. Further constructing a cross-modal semantic alignment and segmentation mechanism using the SPAM module for language-pixel feature collaborative modeling, and the AGMD decoder for anomaly mask generation. Building the Anomaly-Instruct20K instruction dataset to enhance semantic understanding and cross-category generalization, validated on 6 industrial and medical datasets for zero-shot anomaly segmentation."
