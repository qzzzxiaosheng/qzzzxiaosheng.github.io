abstract: "ECA-ResNeXt-8-SVM 是一种面向遥感图像场景分类的轻量化深度学习模型，旨在提升模型对高层语义信息和显著分类特征的提取能力。该方法在 ResNeXt 网络中引入有效通道注意力（ECA）机制，使模型更加关注具有判别性的特征通道，并采用支持向量机（SVM）替代传统全连接层进行分类，从而提升分类精度与泛化能力。实验结果表明，该模型在 UC Merced Land-Use 数据集上取得了 95.81% 的分类准确率，同时显著降低了模型训练时间。"
abstract_en: "ECA-ResNeXt-8-SVM is a lightweight deep learning framework for remote sensing image scene classification, aiming to improve the extraction of high-level semantic and discriminative features. It incorporates the Efficient Channel Attention (ECA) mechanism into ResNeXt to emphasize informative feature channels and employs a Support Vector Machine (SVM) instead of a conventional fully connected layer for classification. Experiments on the UC Merced Land-Use dataset demonstrate that the proposed model achieves 95.81% classification accuracy while significantly reducing training time."
