abstract: "LScAD 是一个面向无监督工业异常检测的大模型–小模型协同框架。该方法利用任务导向的小模型生成异常引导信息，并通过图像和文本提示引导 SAM 进行精确的异常分割。同时，引入颜色域与频率域双分支适配器，增强 SAM 对工业缺陷的领域感知能力。LScAD 在 MVTec AD 及多个真实工业数据集上取得了优异性能，并能够自然扩展到多类别异常检测场景。"
abstract_en: "LScAD is a large–small model collaboration framework for unsupervised industrial anomaly detection. It leverages a task-oriented small model to generate anomaly guidance, which is transformed into image and text prompts to guide SAM for precise anomaly segmentation. A dual-branch adapter operating in the color and frequency domains further enhances SAM’s domain-specific capability for industrial defects. LScAD achieves strong performance on MVTec AD and real-world industrial datasets, while naturally supporting multiclass anomaly detection."
