abstract: "CAPS 是一种面向工业缺陷分割的平移等变性增强方法，旨在提升模型在输入图像发生轻微平移时的输出一致性。该方法设计了新的下采样/上采样模块，通过自适应窗口机制减小边界变化对等变性的影响，并利用分量注意力机制融合多尺度特征，从而兼顾分割精度与平移等变性。实验结果表明，CAPS 在多个工业缺陷数据集上均取得了优异的分割性能和更稳定的输出一致性。"
abstract_en: "CAPS is a shift-equivariance enhancement method for industrial defect segmentation, aiming to improve the consistency of model outputs under small translations of input images. It introduces novel downsampling and upsampling modules with adaptive windowing to reduce the impact of boundary variations, together with a component attention mechanism for effective feature fusion. Experiments on multiple industrial defect datasets demonstrate that CAPS achieves both strong segmentation performance and improved shift equivalence."
