abstract: "ALMRR 是一种面向工业纹理图像无监督异常定位的特征重建与细化方法。该方法利用 Mamba 对语义特征进行重建，并通过人工合成异常为模型引入异常先验，随后采用特征细化模块进一步增强正常与异常区域之间的差异，从而生成更加准确的异常图。实验结果表明，ALMRR 在 MVTec-AD-Textured 及多个真实工业数据集上取得了优异的异常定位性能。"
abstract_en: "ALMRR is a feature reconstruction and refinement framework for unsupervised anomaly localization on industrial textured images. It leverages Mamba to reconstruct semantic features and introduces synthetic anomalies to provide the model with prior knowledge of abnormal patterns. A feature refinement module is further employed to enhance the distinction between normal and anomalous regions for accurate anomaly localization. Experiments on MVTec-AD-Textured and real-world industrial datasets demonstrate the strong performance of ALMRR."
