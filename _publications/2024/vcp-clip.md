abstract: "VCP-CLIP 是一种面向零样本异常分割的视觉上下文提示方法，旨在减少对产品类别信息和人工文本提示设计的依赖。该方法通过 Pre-VCP 模块将全局视觉信息融入文本提示，并利用 Post-VCP 模块根据图像的细粒度特征进一步调整文本表示，从而激活 CLIP 对异常语义的感知能力。实验结果表明，VCP-CLIP 在 10 个真实工业异常分割数据集上取得了优异的零样本异常分割性能。"
abstract_en: "VCP-CLIP is a visual context prompting framework for zero-shot anomaly segmentation, aiming to reduce the reliance on product-specific category information and manually designed text prompts. It introduces a Pre-VCP module to inject global visual context into text prompts and a Post-VCP module to further refine text representations using fine-grained image features, thereby enhancing CLIP’s anomaly perception capability. Experiments on 10 real-world industrial anomaly segmentation datasets demonstrate the strong performance of VCP-CLIP."
