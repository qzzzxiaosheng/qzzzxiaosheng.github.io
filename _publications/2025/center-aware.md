abstract: "CRAS 是一种面向多类别工业异常检测的中心感知残差异常合成方法，旨在通过统一模型同时检测多个类别的异常。该方法利用中心感知残差学习将不同类别的样本映射至统一中心，从而缓解类别间干扰；同时提出距离引导的异常合成策略，根据正常数据分布自适应调整噪声强度，以减少正常与异常样本之间的类内重叠。实验结果表明，CRAS 在多个工业异常检测数据集和真实工业场景中取得了优异的检测性能与具有竞争力的推理效率。"
abstract_en: "CRAS is a Center-aware Residual Anomaly Synthesis framework for multi-class industrial anomaly detection, aiming to detect anomalies across multiple categories with a unified model. It employs center-aware residual learning to map samples from different categories toward a unified center, thereby reducing inter-class interference. A distance-guided anomaly synthesis strategy is further introduced to adaptively adjust noise variance according to the normal data distribution, alleviating the overlap between normal and anomalous samples. Experiments on multiple datasets and real-world industrial applications demonstrate the strong detection performance and competitive inference efficiency of CRAS."
