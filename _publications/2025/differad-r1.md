abstract: "DifferAD-R1 是一种面向工业异常定位的多模态大模型增强强化学习框架，旨在提升模型对跨场景、细粒度异常的定位能力。该方法将异常定位重构为基于双图像的差异定位任务，通过 Difference-Guided 范式显式建模正常与异常图像之间的差异，并设计双一致性定位奖励以增强对细微缺陷的学习信号。此外，引入难度感知的自适应重加权与分组重采样策略，使模型更加关注困难样本。实验结果表明，DifferAD-R1 在工业异常定位任务上显著优于现有方法，并展现出与超大规模视觉语言模型相竞争的性能。"
abstract_en: "DifferAD-R1 is an MLLM-augmented reinforcement learning framework for industrial anomaly localization, aiming to improve the localization of subtle and cross-scenario anomalies. It reformulates anomaly localization as a dual-image difference grounding task, explicitly modeling the discrepancies between normal and anomalous images. A dual-consistency localization reward is introduced to provide more effective learning signals for hard-to-detect defects, together with difficulty-aware adaptive reweighting and group-wise resampling to emphasize challenging samples. Experiments demonstrate that DifferAD-R1 significantly outperforms existing baselines and achieves competitive performance against much larger vision-language models."
