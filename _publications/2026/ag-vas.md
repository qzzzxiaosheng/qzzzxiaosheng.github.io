abstract: "AG-VAS 是一种面向零样本视觉异常分割的锚点引导框架，旨在提升大规模多模态模型对抽象异常概念的理解与精确定位能力。该方法引入三个可学习的语义锚点 [SEG]、[NOR] 和 [ANO]，分别用于显式建模异常区域、正常模式与异常模式之间的语义关系，从而构建统一的异常分割范式。与此同时，AG-VAS 设计了语义-像素对齐模块和锚点引导掩码解码器，以增强高层语义表示与像素级视觉特征之间的对齐，实现更准确的异常定位。实验结果表明，AG-VAS 在多个工业与医学基准上均取得了优异的零样本异常分割性能。"
abstract_en: "AG-VAS is an anchor-guided framework for zero-shot visual anomaly segmentation, aiming to improve large multimodal models in understanding abstract anomaly concepts and accurately localizing anomalous regions. It introduces three learnable semantic anchor tokens, [SEG], [NOR], and [ANO], to explicitly model anomaly regions as well as the semantic contrast between normal and abnormal patterns, forming a unified anomaly segmentation paradigm. To further enhance localization accuracy, AG-VAS incorporates a Semantic-Pixel Alignment Module and an Anchor-Guided Mask Decoder to better align high-level semantic embeddings with pixel-level visual features. Experiments on multiple industrial and medical benchmarks demonstrate the strong zero-shot anomaly segmentation performance of AG-VAS."
