abstract: "CoPS 是一种面向零样本异常检测的条件提示合成框架，旨在通过视觉特征动态生成更加适应不同类别与异常状态的文本提示。该方法从细粒度图像特征中提取正常与异常原型，并将其显式注入提示中，以实现自适应状态建模；同时利用变分自编码器建模语义图像特征，进一步丰富类别相关的提示信息。此外，CoPS 引入空间感知对齐机制，以增强文本表示与局部视觉特征之间的匹配。实验结果表明，CoPS 在 13 个工业和医学数据集上均取得了优异的零样本异常检测与分割性能。"
abstract_en: "CoPS is a conditional prompt synthesis framework for zero-shot anomaly detection, aiming to generate dynamic prompts that adapt to different categories and anomaly states based on visual features. It extracts representative normal and anomaly prototypes from fine-grained image features and explicitly injects them into prompts for adaptive state modeling. A variational autoencoder is further employed to model semantic image features and enrich category-related prompt information. Together with a spatially-aware alignment mechanism, CoPS achieves strong zero-shot anomaly detection and segmentation performance across 13 industrial and medical datasets."
